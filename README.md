## 🚀 What I do

- 🔄 **Data Engineering:** ETL/ELT pipelines with PySpark on AWS, orchestrated with Step Functions, Lambda and EventBridge.
- 🧱 **Data Modelling:** Data Lake and Data Warehouse architectures (S3, Glue, Redshift) and dimensional models ready for analytics.
- 📊 **Business Intelligence:** semantic models, DAX and Power BI dashboards that turn data into decisions.
- 🤖 **Generative AI for data:** multi-agent workflows that automate requirements, ETL, modelling and reporting.
- 🔐 **Data governance & security:** traceability, data quality and anonymisation in regulated (GRC) environments.
